Pioneering Excellence in the Life Science Industry

D

Dr. Abdelhamid Aboelaly

DELTACHEM Executive & Technical Leadership

How DELTACHEM combines global sourcing integrity with compliant warehousing to deliver critical ingredients across Egypt and the MENA region.

The life science sector requires an uncompromised commitment to quality, traceability, and supply chain integrity. As Egypt and the broader Middle East and North Africa (MENA) region expand their domestic manufacturing capacities for pharmaceuticals, nutraceuticals, and veterinary healthcare, the demand for verified, pharmacopoeia-compliant ingredients has never been higher.

Bridging Global Manufacturers and Regional Formulators

DELTACHEM operates as a strategic bridge between internationally audited chemical manufacturers and regional production facilities. Through structured business models—encompassing both authorized commission representation and stock-and-trade operations—we ensure that pharmaceutical manufacturers access certified Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs) and specialty excipients without supply chain bottlenecks.

Compliance, Storage, and Sister-Company Synergy

A critical pillar of DELTACHEM's operational excellence is our dedicated partnership with our sister company, Harvest Pharma. Through Harvest Pharma's state-of-the-art warehousing facilities in Cairo, ingredients are stored under strictly controlled climate conditions, adhering to Good Distribution Practices (GDP) and regional health authority regulations.

From initial Certificate of Analysis (COA) verification and batch audit to climate-controlled delivery, DELTACHEM remains committed to empowering manufacturers with ingredients that safeguard consumer health and foster industrial innovation.
